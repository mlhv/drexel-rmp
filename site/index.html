DU ProfessorView

Professor ratings on Drexel course pages (unofficial)

A Chrome extension that shows Rate My Professors ratings next to instructor names on Drexel's Term Master Schedule and Banner class registration pages. Hover a badge for difficulty, would-take-again percentage, and the number of ratings.

Add to Chrome

How it works

The extension reads instructor names on the Drexel pages you open and looks up their public ratings. It never sees your Drexel password, your schedule, or anything else on the page. Details are in the privacy policy.
